Savage Wheels: licences
=======================

The game: Savage Wheels by KenamicK Entertainment (Peter Petrov and
friends, 2003-2026), https://github.com/kenamick/savagewheels.

- The program is under the MIT License (LICENSE.txt; README-SavageWheels.txt
  and Help.html are upstream's own read-me and game help).
- TinyXML (by Lee Thomason, zlib licence) is built into the program, as
  upstream builds it.
- The game data in Data/ are upstream's own data archive
  (savagewheels-gamedata.tar.gz, released beside the source), unchanged,
  without logo.avi, which the game does not use. The sound effects in
  Data/sound are CC0 (Data/sound/LICENSE lists them); the pictures, cars
  and the four Impulse Tracker tunes come with the game from its authors.
- SDL 2 and SDL2_mixer are GFOpenUp's (LIBS:GFOpenGPU), not in this drawer.
- The Amiga port's own files (amiga/, AMIGA_PORT.md, package/, tools/,
  tests/) are MIT, like the game; the GlowIcon art is The AmiCradle Team's
  own.
